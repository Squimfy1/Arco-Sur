# Arc brand marks

All from Circle's official Arc logo kit (Circle Brand Kit,
https://www.circle.com/pressroom#brandkit, file `Arc_Logos.zip`, fetched
21 Nov 2026), copied unaltered:

- `arc-on-dark.svg`: `Full Logo/SVG/Arc_Logo_White.svg`, for dark backgrounds.
- `arc-on-light.svg`: `Full Logo/SVG/Arc_Logo_Black.svg`, for the light look.
- `arc-network.svg`: `Arc Network Icon/Arc_network.svg`, the network badge in
  the account chip and the recipient list.

Rules we follow (https://www.arc.io/brand-guidelines-and-partner-toolkit):
never recolour, redraw or distort them; no text or graphics on top; keep
clear space; Arc never more prominent than our own brand; say "built on
Arc", never "Arc's" or "Arc" in our product name. Attribution: "Arc is a
trademark of Circle Internet Group, Inc. and/or its affiliates" (in the
legal line at the bottom of every screen). Questions: trademarks@circle.com.

The Cathedral mark (the Sun Door in soft silver) is not a file here: it is
drawn from `../mark.ts` in the text colour by `../Brand.svelte`. The app icon
is `electron/resources/icon.svg` (and its PNG).
